3. Preparation

Examples: degree level, license, certification, internship, related experience, technical training.
How does the preparation required feel to you?

What do the opportunities and conditions look like?

Capture 2 things that matter to you

5. Reality check

Now check real job postings

Your career information shows the occupation broadly. A couple of current postings can show how the work varies by employer.

Find 1–2 postings for this career or a very similar title.
  1. Scan responsibilities. What matches what you already found?
  2. Scan qualifications. What shows up in both postings?
  3. Notice anything new or different—but do not treat one employer’s wish list as a rule for the entire career.
